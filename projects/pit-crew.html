Pit Crew

Overview

Pit Crew is a niche job board for the motorsport and racing industry. It connects job seekers with racing teams and organisations, giving the motorsport community a focused place to find roles and talent.

Key Features

  • Specialized Job Listings: Motorsport-focused job postings
  • Professional Profiles: Profiles showcasing motorsport experience and qualifications
  • Applications: Apply to listings and manage candidates
  • Authentication: Secure sign-in with NextAuth
  • Forms: Smooth listing and application forms built with react-hook-form
  • Clean UI: Interface built with Radix UI components

Technical Stack

  • Framework: Next.js, React, TypeScript
  • Data: Prisma ORM over NeonDB
  • Auth: NextAuth
  • Forms & Validation: react-hook-form, Zod
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS, Radix UI

Architecture

The application follows a modern full-stack Next.js architecture:

  • Frontend: Next.js with React and Radix UI components
  • Data Layer: Prisma ORM over NeonDB (serverless Postgres)
  • Auth: NextAuth for authentication and sessions
  • Forms: react-hook-form with Zod validation at the boundaries

Challenges & Solutions

  • Industry Focus: Built a job board tailored to the motorsport niche
  • Form UX: Used react-hook-form with Zod for reliable, validated submissions
  • Auth: Integrated NextAuth for secure user management
  • User Experience: Designed clear interfaces for both job seekers and teams

Impact & Results

  • Career Opportunities: Connects motorsport professionals with relevant career opportunities
  • Industry Growth: Supports growth of motorsport industry through talent acquisition
  • Professional Development: Provides resources for career advancement in motorsport
  • Community Building: Fosters professional community within motorsport industry
